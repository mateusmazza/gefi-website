Global Executive
Function Initiative

A collaborative network advancing how we understand, assess, and support children’s executive functions around the world.

Our mission

We build research capacity to assess, study, and support executive function skills and behaviors in children ages 3–12, especially in low-resource settings.

GEFI brings together researchers with experience across cultures and communities. We share tools, practical knowledge, and lessons from adapting assessments to local contexts.

Our vision

A global research community that learns together about children’s development and uses that knowledge to inform education, programs, and policy.

Our work connects scientific rigor with the experiences and priorities of the communities where children grow up.
