Our mission

To foster research capacity to assess, study, and promote executive function skills and behaviors in children around the globe.

Shared work

From measurement questions to practical resources.

GEFI combines methodological expertise and local knowledge so that executive function research can be rigorous, meaningful, and useful across settings.
