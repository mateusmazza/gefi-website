Community / General membership

Join the GEFI network.

For researchers, students, and practitioners whose work or interests connect with GEFI’s mission. Membership is free.

What membership offers

  • Connect with people working across cultures and contexts.
  • Exchange resources, practical knowledge, and opportunities.
  • Attend community sessions and propose or join working groups.

Participation is flexible. We plan two 60-minute community sessions each year, alongside activities led by members.

Membership application

Apply for membership.

A short application asks about your work, interests, and connection to GEFI. A publication or referral is welcome but not required.

Working groups

Propose a working group.

Bring together GEFI members around a shared research question, resource, or activity. A proposal needs at least three members, including a lead, and a short charter describing the purpose, intended output, and approximate timeline.
