Community / Steering committee

Steering committee

A single committee guides GEFI’s scientific direction, community activities, and routine decisions.

Role and responsibilities

The Steering Committee oversees membership, working groups, activities, partnerships, and the use of GEFI’s name for collective work. A coordinator supports applications, communications, scheduling, and follow-up.

The committee will include eight to ten members, including at least two junior or early-career members. Its composition will encourage representation from, based in, and working in Majority World settings.

The initial structure will operate as a 24-month pilot.
