Proposed 2026 structure

Founder-led governance, broad participation.

GEFI’s proposed next phase pairs a focused governance structure with an open network for exchange, working groups, and collaboration.

A

Scientific direction

Advisory Board

Senior founding members and a small rotating group of junior advisory members guide scientific priorities, partnerships, and major changes.

  • Up to 18 senior advisory members
  • Up to 5 junior advisory members
  • At least one Advisory Board meeting each year
S

Routine decisions

Steering Committee

A rotating subset of senior advisory members handles membership, working-group charters, annual activities, and use of the GEFI name.

  • 5–6 members
  • Staggered two-year terms
  • Quarterly meetings and asynchronous decisions
N

Community and exchange

GEFI Network

Researchers, students, and practitioners aligned with GEFI’s mission can join activities, find collaborators, share opportunities, and lead working groups.

  • Open application
  • No membership fee
  • Directory, events, opportunities, and working groups
