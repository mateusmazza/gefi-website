Working groups

Build something together

Any Network Member could propose a working group with at least three members, a lead, and a short charter.

  • Clear purpose and intended output
  • Approximate timeline
  • Brief update every six months
  • Archive after 12 months inactive, with the option to restart

A light rhythm

Regular touchpoints without overload.

The initial model focuses on a small number of useful, well-supported activities.

◌

One session per semester

A 60-minute webinar or practical exchange, with rotating chairs and time zones.

↗

Shared opportunities

A moderated channel for relevant jobs, grants, events, training, and collaboration requests.

◎

Local gatherings

Meetings alongside conferences or at partner institutions as capacity and funding allow.
