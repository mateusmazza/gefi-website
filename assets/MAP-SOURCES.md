# Community map sources

Member names, photographs and affiliations: the migrated founding-member biographies in `founding-members.html`, based on GEFI’s approved original website. Data assembled October 24, 2026. Coordinates are approximate institutional bases, not residences or assertions of each person’s current physical location. RTI International members use the institutional headquarters in Research Triangle Park (Durham); Catherine Draper uses her listed University of the Witwatersrand affiliation in Johannesburg.

Land geometry: Natural Earth 1:110m via world-atlas 2 land-110m.json, https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2/land-110m.json . Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . The data was converted locally to SVG, without a runtime mapping service or API key.

Edit `assets/community-members.json` to maintain the data, then synchronize the embedded `community-member-data` JSON in `community.html`. Photos remain in `assets/legacy/`. Only expressly approved public information belongs here; never copy application responses or emails into the website.

Stanford identity assets: `stanford-wordmark.png` is the original GEFI/Stanford lockup from the existing approved website. The Stanford logo font is the unmodified https://www-media.stanford.edu/assets/fonts/stanford.woff file referenced by the legacy site stylesheet. Header/footer guidance: https://identity.stanford.edu/digital/web-design/brand-compliance/ and https://decanter.stanford.edu/examples/global-footer .
