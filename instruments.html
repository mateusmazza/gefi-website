EFFORT

Executive Function from Observation and Reflection Tool

We developed and validated the Executive Function from Observation and Reflection Tool (EFFORT), a publicly available, open-source item bank designed for cultural adaptation. It includes 32 parallel items for caregivers and teachers. Each item consists of a stem that describes a universally relevant EF behavior, along with culturally relevant examples of activities and situations that elicit these behaviors. EFFORT also includes 10 assessor report items for use following a standardized assessment session. All items, adaptation guidelines, translated versions, and culturally-relevant examples can be accessed here: https://osf.io/k3edb.

Assessment guidance

Direct EF assessment guidelines

We compiled recommended practices for selection, adaptation, and administration of EF performance-based tasks and for processing, evaluating, reporting, and interpreting data that these tasks yield. We hope this resource will help researchers conduct more psychometrically rigorous and ecologically valid assessments of children’s EF skills, expanding scientific understanding of these skills across different cultures and contexts.
